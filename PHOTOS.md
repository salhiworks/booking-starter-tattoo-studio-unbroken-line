# Photos — Ligne Tattoo (Unbroken Line)

One studio, one light: a single daylit shoot by **Michael Burrows**, Pexels ids 7147700–7147841 — a
fine-line tattoo artist in a white room with drawings taped to the wall, drawing on a tablet and on
paper at a pale wooden desk, printing and placing violet transfer stencils, tattooing in black gloves,
and the fresh line work on a client's side, shoulder blade and arm. Every frame shows **one artist
and at most one client**.

Licence: the **Pexels License** (free to use, no attribution required; not to be sold unaltered or
used to imply endorsement). Credit is given here anyway.

These are **stock photographs, not work by the sample studio**. The site labels every frame "Sample
photo". Never describe them as Ligne's studio, its artist, a client or the studio's tattoos, and never
call the tattoos in them healed — the work shown is fresh.

Processed by `concepts/tattoo-studio-unbroken-line/photo-pipeline/photos.mjs` (crop + one shared
grade: the shoot's blue-grey whites pulled a touch toward neutral wall white, saturation held back
slightly so the violet stencil lines stay the brightest colour; no retouching). Only the processed
AVIF/WebP crops are in `public/photos/` — no originals.
